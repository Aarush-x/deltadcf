## Summary
The application features a highly intentional, terminal inspired aesthetic that suits an equity research tool. It avoids common "AI slop" patterns like purple gradients and bubbly buttons, opting instead for sharp corners, mono typography, and data dense layouts. However, there are significant issues with font delivery and minor inconsistencies in spacing and light mode contrast.

---

## Findings

### FINDING-001: Font Delivery Failure (Critical)
- **Category**: Typography
- **Severity**: High
- **Description**: The configured brand fonts (Hanken Grotesk and JetBrains Mono) are not loading, causing the browser to fall back to Times or system-ui. This significantly degrades the intended professional/technical aesthetic.
- **Impact**: High (Breaks the brand identity and terminal feel)
- **Recommendation**: Add the Google Fonts import to `index.html` or `App.jsx`.

### FINDING-002: Pure Black Background Eye Strain (Medium)
- **Category**: Color & Contrast
- **Severity**: Medium
- **Description**: The dark mode uses #000000. While fitting for a terminal look, it creates high contrast "bloom" on OLED screens and can be fatiguing for long research sessions.
- **Recommendation**: Soften the background to a deep gray (e.g., #0B0B0B or #121212).

### FINDING-003: Light Mode Hierarchy Compression (Medium)
- **Category**: Color & Contrast / Hierarchy
- **Severity**: Medium
- **Description**: In light mode, the "Intrinsic Value" section and "Integrity Alerts" lose their depth. The borders feel heavier than the content.
- **Recommendation**: Use subtle background elevations (off-whites) instead of relying solely on #1F1F1F borders for separation.

### FINDING-004: Inconsistent Section Spacing (Polish)
- **Category**: Spacing & Layout
- **Severity**: Polish
- **Description**: The "Quantitative Checklist" and "Core Business Audit" are tightly packed, while the Intrinsic Value section has generous internal padding.
- **Recommendation**: Normalize the vertical rhythm across all analysis components.
